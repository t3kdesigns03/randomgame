How to play

  • Hit the ball over the net so it bounces on the bot's side.
  • The ball must bounce in the court lines on their side — wall first, out, or into the net is a fault.
  • If it bounces twice on their side before they return it, it's your point.
  • Serve: the ball drops on the server's side — hit it over. Serve swaps every 2 points.
  • Jump into the ball to lift it. Boost pads refill your boost.
